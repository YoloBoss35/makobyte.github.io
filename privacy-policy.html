1. Privacy Policy

Summary: The income and expense records you enter never reach us; they are not sent to our servers and we have no access to them. The app does not ask you to create an account. If it is turned on on your phone, Android's own backup system backs your records up to your Google account. Apart from that, the only data collected is technical data used to show ads.

Where are your records kept?

All income, expense, account balance, gold/currency holding, credit card and instalment loan debt, time deposit, subscription and settings information you enter in the app is stored in your phone's own memory. These records are not transmitted to us, not transferred to our servers, and we have no access to them.

Everyday use of the app does not require an internet connection. The internet is used only to fetch current currency/gold rates and to show ads.

Google backup

The Android operating system offers a feature that backs app data up to the user's own Google account. This feature is enabled for this app. Its purpose is to keep your budget records from being lost if you lose or replace your phone.

  • If Google backup is turned on on your phone, your budget records are backed up to your own Google account.
  • This backup is encrypted by Google; we cannot access it. The backup belongs to you, not to us.
  • When you move to a new phone, your records can be restored.
  • If you do not want this, you can turn it off in your phone's Settings > Google > Backup.

Important: If Google backup is turned off, there is no other copy of your records. In that case, if you delete the app or lose your phone, your records cannot be recovered — we do not hold a backup. We recommend creating your own backup file using Settings > Back Up in the app.

Automatic expense detection (optional)

The app has an optional feature that automatically picks up your expenses from bank notifications. This feature is off by default and works only if you turn it on.

When you turn it on from Settings > Notification Settings > Automatic Expense Detection, Android asks you for the "notification access" permission. Once you grant it, the app does the following:

  • It reads the text of notifications arriving on your phone and looks for a spending expression (Turkish banking phrases such as “harcama” (expense), “ödeme” (payment), “pos işlemi” (card payment)) together with a TRY amount.
  • If it finds a matching notification, it extracts the amount and the bank name and shows you a confirmation screen. No expense is added unless you confirm it.
  • Notifications captured while the app is closed are kept temporarily on the phone until you open the app.

The following notifications are never processed: messaging, social media and e-mail apps (such as WhatsApp, Telegram, BiP, Signal, Messenger, Instagram, Facebook, X, TikTok, YouTube, Gmail); shopping, marketplace and price-comparison apps (such as Trendyol, Hepsiburada, Getir, Akakçe, Cimri); and system notifications.

For SMS and conversation-style notifications, only the sender name is checked first. If the sender is not a bank, the message text is never touched; only the text of messages from banks is read. The app does not ask for permission to read SMS.

Data obtained this way does not leave your phone. It is not sent to us, not transmitted to any server and not shared with ad networks. Notification texts that are read are not included in Google backup; an expense you confirm, however, becomes part of your budget records and, together with your other records, is included in that backup if Google backup is turned on on your phone, as described in the “Google backup” section above. You can withdraw the permission at any time from your phone's notification access settings; once withdrawn, the feature stops completely.

Ads

The app is free and is supported by ads shown through Google AdMob. To be able to show ads, AdMob automatically collects the following technical data from your device and shares it with Google:

  • Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID – AAID)
  • Your IP address (to determine your approximate region)
  • Your ad interactions (impressions, clicks, viewing time)
  • Diagnostic data (app and SDK performance: launch time, hang rate, energy use)

This data is transferred to Google's servers abroad. If you allow personalised ads, it is also shared, to the extent you allow, with Google's advertising partners; you can see the full list of partners in Settings > Ad Preferences. Your budget records are not part of this transfer.

Whether personalised ads are shown, and whether data is transferred abroad for that purpose, is your choice; the app asks you about it separately on first launch. Even if you do not allow it, you can use every feature of the app in full — the ads are simply not personalised. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad Preferences. For details, see the Explicit Consent Statements section.

Users in the European Union/European Economic Area and the United Kingdom are asked this question through Google's certified consent management platform, and their choice is stored there; for details see the EU/EEA and UK users section.

To reset your advertising ID or turn off ad tracking completely, you can also use your device's Settings > Google > Ads.

When you export your data yourself

The app lets you export your records as an Excel file or a backup file. You start this action and you decide where to send the file (e-mail, cloud storage, a messaging app). What happens to the file after it leaves your phone is outside our control.

You can share the technical error log under Settings › Report a Problem in the same way. This log does not contain information such as amounts, account names, notes or merchant names; it contains only the time, source and type of the error and technical trace information.

Third-party services used

  • Google AdMob — Used to show ads; processes the technical data listed above. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Android Auto Backup (Google) — If turned on on your phone, backs your budget records up to your Google account.
  • Truncgil Finans (finans.truncgil.com) — Used only to fetch current currency and gold rates. None of your records or personal information is sent to this service; only the rate list is requested. During the connection your IP address reaches the relevant server as a technical necessity.

Apart from these, the app contains no component that collects usage statistics or tracks you.

Permissions requested

  • Internet — for rate information and showing ads
  • Sending notifications — for limit warnings, the daily reminder, subscription payment reminders and due-date reminders for planned payments
  • Setting alarms and running after restart — so that scheduled reminders keep working after the phone restarts
  • Access to the advertising ID — so that Google AdMob can show ads (Android Advertising ID; see the “Ads” section above)
  • Biometric authentication — only if you turn on the App Lock feature; authentication is handed over to your phone's own screen lock, and fingerprint or face data never reaches the app and is not stored
  • Notification access — only if you turn on the Automatic Expense Detection feature; not requested by default

The app does not request access to your contacts, location, SMS messages, camera or gallery.

Security

Your records are stored in the app's area on your phone, where other apps cannot access them. However, the security of your device is largely in your hands: we recommend using a screen lock on your phone and downloading the app only from the official app store.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always published on this page and the date of the last update is shown above.

It is, however, a technical necessity that a limited amount of technical data (such as your IP address) reaches Google's infrastructure so that ads can be served and ad fraud can be prevented.

How long the budget records on your device are kept is entirely under your control. Notification data captured through automatic detection is deleted from the device after you confirm or reject it. 5. Deleting your data

This section explains how to delete data for the Budget Tracker app (com.makobyte.budget_tracker, developer: Berkay Karlı).

What to know first: The app does not require you to create an account and has no server. All the budget records you enter are stored only on your own phone. The developer has no access to these records and there is no account or record of yours on the developer's side that needs to be deleted. You therefore delete the data yourself, and there is no request you need to wait to be approved.

5.1. Deleting your data within the app (recommended)

  1. Open the app.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Reset Data.
  4. Read the warning and confirm.

This action cannot be undone and takes effect as soon as it is completed.

5.2. Deleting by uninstalling the app

Uninstalling the app from your phone also deletes all data stored on the device. If a backup has been created in your Google account, see 5.5.

5.3. What data is deleted?

With either of the two methods above, all of the following is deleted:

  • Your income and expense records
  • Your accounts, balances and transaction ledger
  • Your gold and currency holding records
  • Your subscriptions, loans, time deposits and recurring records
  • The categories you created, your budget limits and your warning threshold
  • Bank notifications awaiting confirmation
  • The technical error log kept on the device
  • Temporary files created during export

Reset Data does not delete appearance and permission preferences such as your theme, app lock, daily reminder, notification and ad preferences. These are not financial data; if you want to remove them completely, you can uninstall the app (5.2).

5.4. Data kept on the developer's side and retention period

Apart from e-mails you send us, no data of yours is kept on the developer's side; so there is no additional retention period after deletion. There is no server, user account or user database.

If you send us a request by e-mail, only the correspondence itself (your e-mail address and your message) remains in our mailbox. We delete this correspondence within 12 months at the latest after your request has been concluded; if you want it deleted sooner, just write to the same address.

5.5. Your Google backup

Depending on your device settings, Android may back up a copy of the app data to your own Google account. This backup is held by Google in your account, not by us. To delete it, remove the relevant backup from your phone's Settings > Google > Backup or from your Google account's backup settings.

5.6. Ad data

The Android Advertising ID used for showing ads in the app is under Google's control, not ours. You can reset this ID or turn off ad tracking completely in your phone's Settings > Google > Ads. You can change your ad consent in the app under Settings > Ad Preferences. Details for EU/EEA and UK users are in section 4.

5.7. Help

If you have trouble deleting your data, you can write to makobyte@hotmail.com. We will reply to your request within 30 days at the latest.
